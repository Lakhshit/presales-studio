Why the numbers hold up

An estimate you can defend line by line

  • 1
    Traceable quantitiesServers, composites, integrations, forms: every number shows whether it came from the RFP, discovery or the customer. Defaults are flagged until confirmed.
  • 2
    Three-point effortOptimistic, most-likely and pessimistic days per unit, calibrated from your team's actuals, combined with PERT.
  • 3
    Confidence, not paddingUncertainty rolls up statistically to P80. Known risks are priced separately as probability × impact.
  • 4
    Quality gatePM overhead, testing share, contingency range, unsourced drivers and wide ranges are checked before anyone can approve.
